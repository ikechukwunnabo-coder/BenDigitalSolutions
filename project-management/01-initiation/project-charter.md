## PART A: PROJECT CHARTER

### 1. Project Overview
Ben Digital Solutions is a digital technology business initiative focused on providing website development, business automation, data analytics, software solutions, digital products and technology consulting services to small and medium-sized businesses.
This project involves planning, designing, developing, testing and launching the company's digital business platform.
The platform will serve as the company's primary digital presence and establish the operational foundation for customer acquisition, service delivery, business administration and future digital revenue streams.
The project will also serve as a practical application of project management methodologies acquired through the Google Project Management Professional Certificate.

### 2. Business Case and Justification
Many small and medium-sized businesses require affordable, reliable and integrated digital solutions to improve their operations, visibility, customer engagement and decision-making.
Ben Digital Solutions intends to address these needs by combining software development, IT infrastructure knowledge, data analytics and business automation.
The project is justified by the following opportunities:

- Establishing a professional digital business presence.
- Creating a platform for attracting and managing prospective customers.
- Developing reusable digital solutions for SMEs.
- Establishing a foundation for recurring revenue.
- Demonstrating practical software development and project management capabilities.
- Creating a scalable business model with multiple potential income streams.

### 3. Project Purpose
To establish a professional, functional and scalable digital business platform that supports the operations, customer engagement and commercial activities of Ben Digital Solutions.

### 4. Project Objectives
The project will pursue the following measurable objectives:

- Complete project initiation, planning and architecture documentation within the first two weeks.
- Design and develop a responsive public-facing business website.
- Establish a Django-based backend and PostgreSQL database.
- Develop a customer enquiry and lead management system.
- Establish an internal administrative dashboard for managing enquiries, services and projects.
- Implement basic project and customer tracking functionality.
- Complete functional testing, security checks and user acceptance testing before launch.
- Deploy the initial platform to a production environment.
- Produce a complete project management portfolio documenting the project lifecycle.
- Establish a foundation for future digital products, automation, analytics and subscription services.

### 5. Project Scope
**In Scope — Initial Minimum Viable Product (MVP)**

#### A. Public Website

- Homepage.
- About the company.
- Services and solutions.
- Portfolio and completed projects.
- Contact page.
- Customer enquiry forms.
- Responsive mobile and desktop layouts.
- Basic search engine optimisation.

#### B. Backend and Database

- Django application.
- Django REST Framework API.
- PostgreSQL database.
- Authentication and authorisation.
- API documentation.
- Data validation and error handling.

#### C. Administrative Platform

- Secure administrative access.
- Lead and enquiry management.
- Customer records.
- Service management.
- Basic project records and status tracking.
- Dashboard summaries.

#### D. Project Management and Documentation

- Project charter.
- Project management plan.
- Work Breakdown Structure.
- Project schedule.
- Budget tracker.
- Risk register.
- Stakeholder and communication register.
- Issue and change logs.
- Progress reports.
- Testing and acceptance records.
- Project closure report.
**Out of Scope — Initial MVP**
The following features are deferred to subsequent development phases:

- Full e-commerce marketplace.
- Advanced subscription management.
- Complete online learning management system.
- Complex AI-powered automation.
- Advanced business intelligence and reporting.
- Multi-tenant SaaS architecture.
- Mobile applications.
- Complex third-party integrations not essential to the MVP.
These features may be introduced through approved future project phases.

### 9. Initial Budget Framework
The initial business development budget is ₦50,000–₦150,000.
The project will prioritise low-cost development tools, free development environments and locally hosted testing.
Budget categories:

- Domain registration.
- VPS hosting and deployment.
- Internet and connectivity.
- Development and testing resources.
- Backup and operational expenses.
- Contingency reserve.
No domain or hosting purchase is authorised by this charter. These expenses require a separate cost review and approval before commitment.
The detailed project budget will be established during the planning phase.

### 10. Project Constraints

- Limited initial financial resources.
- Solo project management and development capacity.
- Approximately 10–15 hours of available work per week.
- Windows 11 development environment.
- Laptop with 8GB RAM.
- Dependence on internet connectivity.
- Technical learning and implementation occurring alongside project delivery.

### 11. Project Assumptions

- Required development tools will be installed and configured.
- AI tools will be available to support development and documentation.
- The project will initially operate as a modular monolithic application.
- Django, Django REST Framework, React, TypeScript and PostgreSQL will form the primary technology stack.
- The MVP will prioritise essential business functionality over advanced features.
- External hosting and domain procurement will take place after local development and readiness checks.

### 12. High-Level Project Risks
Initial risks include:

- Scope expansion and uncontrolled feature requests.
- Development delays caused by technical difficulties.
- Insufficient time for testing and documentation.
- Budget overruns.
- Data security vulnerabilities.
- Internet or power interruptions.
- Dependency on AI-generated code without sufficient verification.
- Limited early customer validation.
Detailed assessment and mitigation actions will be maintained in the Risk Register.

### 13. Project Success Criteria
The project will be considered successful when:

- The agreed MVP scope is delivered.
- All critical features pass functional testing.
- No unresolved critical security or data-integrity issues remain.
- The website works across supported mobile and desktop screen sizes.
- Customer enquiries can be captured and managed.
- The administrative platform functions as intended.
- The system is successfully deployed and accessible.
- The project documentation is complete.
- Actual expenditure is documented and compared against the approved budget.
- A formal project closure review is completed.
